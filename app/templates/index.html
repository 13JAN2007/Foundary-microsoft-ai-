The 6 Principles of Responsible AI (Microsoft Standard v2)
Audit AI scenarios and detect algorithmic bias, safety vulnerabilities, and PII leakage
Week 1 Focus
1. Fairness

Treat all demographic groups equitably without systemic bias across gender, ethnicity, or socioeconomic status.

2. Reliability & Safety

Operate reliably within design boundaries, handle edge cases gracefully, and prevent hazardous hallucinations.

3. Privacy & Security

Safeguard personal data (PII), respect consent, prevent prompt-injection attacks, and maintain encryption.

4. Inclusiveness

Empower people of diverse physical abilities, languages, and cultural backgrounds through accessible design (WCAG).

5. Transparency

Provide explainable outputs, model cards, confidence indicators, and clear disclosures that an AI is responding.

6. Accountability

Designated human operators must remain answerable for system decisions (Human-in-the-loop oversight).
